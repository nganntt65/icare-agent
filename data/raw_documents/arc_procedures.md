# Alien Resident Certificate (ARC) Guidelines for International Students in Taiwan

## Overview
All international students holding a Resident Visa must apply for an Alien Resident Certificate (ARC / 外僑居留證) within 30 days of arrival in Taiwan. The ARC serves as your official legal identification and residence permit in Taiwan.

## Critical Deadlines
- **Resident Visa Holders**: Must apply for an ARC within **30 days** of entry into Taiwan.
- **Visitor Visa Holders**: Must apply for a visitor visa extension within **15 days** of arrival or apply to convert to an ARC at the National Immigration Agency before your visitor visa expires, provided you have official university enrollment documentation.
- **ARC Renewal / Extension**: You must submit your renewal application within **30 days prior to the expiration date** printed on your ARC card.
- **Overstay Penalties**: Overstaying your visa or ARC will result in severe penalties under Taiwan immigration law, including fines ranging from NT$2,000 to NT$10,000, deportation, and a travel ban.

## Required Documents Checklist
Prepare the following verified documents before visiting the National Immigration Agency (NIA) or submitting via the online portal:
1. **Application Form**: Completed ARC application form (with one 2-inch color photo with white background taken within 6 months).
2. **Passport**: Original passport with valid entry visa and entry stamp, plus one photocopy of the identity page and visa page.
3. **Student Status Verification**: Official Certificate of Enrollment (在學證明) or official Admission Letter issued by the Office of International Affairs (OIA).
4. **Proof of Accommodation**: On-campus dormitory residency certificate (obtainable from Dorm Manager) or off-campus housing lease contract.
5. **Application Fee**: NT$1,000 for a 1-year student ARC (payable by cash, credit card, or convenience store payment code if applying online).

## National Immigration Agency (NIA) Service Center
- **Office Location**: Taipei City Service Center, National Immigration Agency
  - Address: No. 15, Guangzhou St., Zhongzheng District, Taipei City (Near MRT Xiaonanmen Station, Exit 2)
- **Service Hours**: Monday to Friday, 08:00 – 17:00 (Continuous service during lunch hours)
- **Phone Consultation**: (02) 2388-9393
- **Foreigners In Taiwan 24h Toll-Free Helpline**: 1990 (Multi-language assistance in English, Vietnamese, Indonesian, Japanese, and Chinese).

## How to Obtain OIA Verification
Visit the Office of International Affairs (OIA), Administration Building Room 201, during counter hours (Monday to Friday, 09:00 – 12:00, 13:30 – 17:00) with your student ID card or admission letter to print your Enrollment Certificate.
