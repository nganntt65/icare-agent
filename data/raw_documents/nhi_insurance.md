# National Health Insurance (NHI) and Medical Insurance in Taiwan

## Overview
Taiwan's National Health Insurance (NHI / 全民健康保險) is a mandatory universal healthcare program providing affordable, high-quality medical coverage. International students residing in Taiwan must enroll in NHI once eligibility requirements are met.

## Eligibility Criteria & 6-Month Waiting Period
Under the National Health Insurance Act:
- International students who hold a valid Alien Resident Certificate (ARC) and have resided in Taiwan continuously for **6 full months** (180 days) are legally obligated and eligible to join NHI.
- **Continuous Residency Rule**: During the 6-month period, you are permitted to leave Taiwan only once, and the duration of the trip must not exceed 30 days. The days spent abroad do not count toward the 180-day total and will delay your eligibility date accordingly.

## First 6 Months: Campus Group Medical Insurance
- During the initial 6 months before qualifying for NHI, international students are automatically enrolled in the **Campus International Student Group Medical Insurance** (administered through Cathay / Fubon Insurance via the Office of International Affairs).
- **Premium**: Approximately NT$3,000 for the 6-month period (included in your initial semester tuition and fee billing slip).
- **Coverage**: Covers emergency room treatments, accident hospitalization, and qualified outpatient doctor visits with reimbursement claims submitted through the Campus Health Center.

## NHI Premium and Payment Method
- **Monthly Fee**: The standard student NHI premium is **NT$826 per month**.
- **Payment Method**: The university OIA collects the NHI premium as part of your semester tuition bill (NT$4,956 for 6 months per semester).
- **Physical NHI Card (健保卡)**: Once your 6-month eligibility is reached, OIA registers your roster with the National Health Insurance Administration. Your physical IC card will be ready for pickup at OIA Room 201 within 2 to 3 weeks after submission.

## Medical Visits & Clinic Copayments
- When seeking medical attention at clinics or hospitals, present your NHI Card and ARC.
- **Typical Copayments**:
  - Local Clinics (診所): NT$150 to NT$200 per visit (includes basic consultation and 3-day medication).
  - Regional Hospitals: NT$240 to NT$400.
  - Medical Centers (e.g., NTUH, Mackay): NT$420 to NT$570.
- Dental checkups (teeth cleaning every 6 months) and preventative care are covered under NHI with minimal registration fees.
