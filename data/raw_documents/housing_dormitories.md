# On-Campus Dormitory and Student Housing Regulations

## Dormitory Check-in Procedures
Incoming international students assigned to on-campus housing must complete the following check-in steps upon arrival:
1. **Reporting to Dorm Desk**: Visit the 1st Floor Dormitory Management Office of your assigned dormitory building.
2. **Required Documents**:
   - Valid Student ID or University Admission Notice.
   - Government-issued ID or Passport.
   - Payment receipt of dormitory fee.
   - Official Health Examination Form B completion stamp or hospital appointment confirmation.
3. **Room Key & Access Card**: Upon signing the Dormitory Regulations Agreement, you will receive your room key and magnetic electronic access card.

## Dormitory Fees and Deposit
- **Undergraduate Dormitories**: NT$12,500 – NT$15,000 per semester (4-person shared room, air conditioning metered separately via campus prepaid card).
- **Graduate Dormitories**: NT$18,000 – NT$24,000 per semester (2-person shared room or single studio).
- **Housing Security Deposit**: NT$2,000 paid at check-in. The deposit is fully refundable at checkout if room equipment is intact and cleaned.

## Access Rules, Curfew, and Quiet Hours
- **Electronic Gate Access**: Dorm exterior doors are locked 24/7 with RFID card access.
- **Quiet Hours**: 23:00 to 06:00 daily. Noise must be kept to a minimum in rooms and hallways.
- **Visitor Policy**: External visitors and non-resident guests are permitted in 1st-floor public lounges between 09:00 and 21:00 only. Overnight stays by visitors are strictly prohibited and result in immediate revocation of dormitory privileges.
- **Prohibited Items**: High-power electrical heating appliances (hot plates, induction cookers, electric heaters), pets, and smoking are strictly forbidden.

## Dormitory Management & Emergency Contacts
- **Resident Assistant (RA) On-Duty**: Located in Room 101 on each building's ground floor.
- **24-Hour Dormitory Front Desk**: Campus phone extension #3100.
- **Campus 24/7 Emergency Security Office**: (02) 2362-0000 or campus extension #5555.

## Off-Campus Housing Assistance
For students preferring off-campus rentals:
- The Office of International Affairs (OIA) maintains a vetted housing database certified by the Tsuei Ma Ma Foundation (崔媽媽基金會).
- Standard Taipei single suite rental market price: NT$9,000 – NT$16,000/month.
- Legal lease advice and contract review assistance are available at the Student Housing Service Section (Administration Building Room 105).
