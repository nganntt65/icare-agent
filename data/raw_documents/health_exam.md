# Mandatory Health Examination Guidelines for International Students

## Legal Regulation and Purpose
In compliance with Taiwan Ministry of Education and Ministry of Health and Welfare regulations, all newly enrolled degree-seeking and exchange international students must complete the **Health Examination Form B (外籍人士居留或定居健康檢查項目表)** within 14 days of entry into Taiwan. Passing the health examination is mandatory for completing official university registration and obtaining an Alien Resident Certificate (ARC).

## Required Examination Items
1. **Chest X-Ray for Pulmonary Tuberculosis**: Mandatory digital radiography.
2. **Serological Test for Syphilis**: Blood screening (RPR/VDRL or TPPA).
3. **Proof of Immunity to Measles and Rubella**:
   - Laboratory antibody titer certificate (positive IgG result), OR
   - Official childhood/booster vaccination certificate proving at least one dose of MMR vaccine.
   - If negative or without documentation, you must receive an on-site MMR vaccination at the hospital.
4. **Physical and Clinical Examination**: Blood pressure, vision, hearing, heart, lungs, and abdomen examination.
5. **Stool Examination for Parasites**: (Required for students from designated regions).

## Fee and Processing Timeline
- **Hospital Exam Fee**: Approximately **NT$1,500 – NT$2,200** (depending on whether MMR vaccination is needed). Paid directly to the hospital cashier.
- **Report Turnaround Time**: Takes **7 to 10 working days** for the official hospital-sealed Health Examination Certificate to be issued. Fast-track options may be requested for an additional fee.

## Submission to Campus Health Center
1. Pick up the original sealed Health Examination Report from the hospital.
2. Submit one original report and one photocopy to the **Campus Health Center** (Student Activity Center Building B, Room 102).
3. The Health Center issues a verification stamp on your Student Registration Check Sheet, allowing the Office of International Affairs (OIA) to issue your permanent Student ID Card.
