# Course Selection and Academic Registration Guidelines

## Academic Course Selection Cycles
Course registration is conducted via the online Academic Course Information Portal in three structured stages:

### Stage 1: Preliminary Course Pre-Registration
- **Timeframe**: Held approximately 3 to 4 weeks prior to semester start.
- **Scope**: Enrollment in required departmental courses, core language modules, and university general education electives.
- **Mechanism**: Computerized randomized lottery for oversubscribed courses. Registration order does not affect lottery odds.

### Stage 2: Second Registration Stage
- **Timeframe**: Held 1 week before classes begin.
- **Scope**: Enrollment in remaining slots, elective courses, and adjustments after Stage 1 results are released.

### Add/Drop Adjustment Period (First 2 Weeks of Semester)
- **Timeframe**: Starts on the first day of class and ends on the Friday of Week 2 at 17:00.
- **Online Add/Drop**: Students can directly add or drop available courses online with remaining vacancies.
- **Manual Instructor Override (加簽 - Instructor Signature Approval)**:
  - When an essential course is full, students must attend the first class session in person.
  - Ask the instructor for an **Add/Drop Authorization Code** or have the instructor physically sign the paper Add/Drop Approval Form.
  - The signed form must be verified and submitted to the Division of Academic Affairs (教務處 / Office of Academic Affairs) counter before the Friday 17:00 Week 2 deadline.

## Credit Hour Requirements
- **Undergraduate International Students**:
  - Minimum credit load: **9 credits** per semester.
  - Maximum credit load: **25 credits** per semester.
- **Master's and PhD Students**:
  - Minimum credit load: **1 course / 3 credits** per semester (prior to thesis defense stage).
  - Maximum credit load: **15 credits** per semester.
- Failure to maintain minimum credit requirements without official dean approval may jeopardize your valid student visa status.

## Academic Tutoring and Support
- **International Peer Tutoring**: Free weekly 1-on-1 tutoring in Calculus, General Physics, and Chinese language provided by the Center for Teaching and Learning Development (CTLD).
- **Academic Affairs Division Counter**: Administration Building Room 102, Hours: Monday – Friday, 08:30 – 17:00.
